Rijksmuseum Game

2D & 3D Game · Team Project · Unity

Project Classified is a 2D and 3D game created around the Rijksmuseum. The main idea was that the player accidentally bumps into a painting while visiting a museum. After making contact with the painting, the player is suddenly transported into the world inside the artwork.

Trapped inside this mysterious world, the player has to explore the environment, overcome obstacles, and find a way to escape back to reality. The game combines elements of both 2D and 3D gameplay to create the experience.

This was a group project consisting of three Game Art students and two Software Development students. One of the requirements was that the game had to be connected to the Rijksmuseum. We had only two weeks to come up with an idea, develop the game, create the assets, and finish a playable version.

I worked on the project using Unity. Because of the limited development time, teamwork and communication were especially important. We had to divide the tasks efficiently and make decisions quickly in order to finish the game within the deadline.

What did I learn?

  • Working on a game within a very limited timeframe
  • Working together with Game Art and Software Development students
  • Developing a game in Unity
  • Combining 2D and 3D elements in a game
  • Dividing tasks and working efficiently as a team
  • Making quick design and development decisions

My Development

The biggest challenge of this project was the two-week deadline. Unlike longer projects, there was very little time to experiment or completely redesign parts of the game. This taught me how important it is to focus on the most important features first and keep the scope manageable.

Working with students from different disciplines also taught me how important communication and teamwork are during game development. The Game Art students and Software Development students each had different responsibilities, so we had to work closely together to combine the visuals and gameplay into one finished game.
